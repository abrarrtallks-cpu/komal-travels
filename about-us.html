Who We Are

Welcome to Komal Travels Kerala, your trusted travel partner for discovering Kerala, God's Own Country. Founded by Komal Suresh and based in Fort Kochi, we've spent 8+ years creating memorable holidays for families, couples, honeymooners, friends and groups.

From the backwaters of Alleppey and the misty hills of Munnar to the wildlife of Thekkady, the heritage of Kochi, the beaches of Kerala, and the natural beauty of Wayanad, we help travellers experience Kerala with comfortable travel arrangements and personalized service.

We understand that every traveller is different — a honeymoon couple wants privacy and romance, a family wants comfortable hotels and easy sightseeing, a group needs larger vehicles and coordinated routes. We avoid a one-size-fits-all approach: we listen to your requirements and build a Kerala itinerary around your travel style, duration, budget, interests and group size.

Our promise is simple: to make your Kerala holiday comfortable, well-planned, hassle-free and truly memorable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Which is the best month to book a Kerala tour package?

Ans: The ideal time to visit Kerala is from September to March, when the weather is generally pleasant and cooler. June to August can suit budget travellers looking for seasonal discounts, and travellers interested in traditional monsoon Ayurveda experiences.

Q. What is the average cost of a budget Kerala tour package?

Ans: A standard 4-Night/5-Day budget Kerala package covering Munnar and Alleppey typically ranges from ₹8,500 to ₹15,000 per person, depending on transport choices, accommodation and meal plans. Prices vary by travel dates and inclusions.

Q. Do your packages include private pick-up from Kochi Airport?

Ans: Yes. Itineraries can feature a dedicated, chauffeur-driven vehicle for airport transfers from Kochi, Trivandrum or Calicut, subject to the selected package and vehicle availability.

Q. Can we combine budget hotels with a luxury houseboat?

Ans: Yes. You can choose suitable 3-star or boutique hotels for hill-station stays and upgrade to a premium private houseboat cruise in Alleppey. We balance accommodation, transportation and experiences around your budget.

Q. What destinations can I cover with Komal Travels Kerala?

Ans: We plan trips covering Kerala's most popular destinations, including Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey, Kochi, Kumarakom, Kovalam, Varkala and Wayanad, and can also arrange combinations reaching into neighbouring South India.

Q. Will all the sightseeing mentioned in my package be covered?

Ans: Yes. Every sightseeing stop in your confirmed itinerary is covered. Our routes are planned so you can comfortably fit in the maximum number of attractions.

Q. Will I have a dedicated vehicle and driver for my trip?

Ans: Yes, depending on your confirmed package we arrange a dedicated vehicle with a professional driver, giving you more comfort and flexibility than travelling between destinations on your own.

Q. Will the driver speak Hindi or English?

Ans: Yes, Hindi- and English-speaking drivers can be arranged, which makes things easier for families and first-time visitors to Kerala.

Q. Can the driver help with more than just driving?

Ans: Our drivers regularly offer local guidance — restaurant recommendations, sightseeing tips and shopping suggestions — to make the trip easier and more enjoyable.

Q. What if I want to visit a place that isn't in my package?

Ans: Let our driver or travel team know. If time, route and local conditions allow, we'll do our best to accommodate it.

Q. Is Komal Travels suitable for families and senior citizens?

Ans: Yes, we regularly plan holidays for families, couples, senior citizens and groups, adjusting the pace, sightseeing and hotel choices to suit the group's comfort.

Q. Can you arrange a honeymoon package?

Ans: Yes, we can put together a romantic itinerary across Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey and other scenic spots, with suitable hotels and special arrangements for your budget.

Q. Can I choose the hotels in my package?

Ans: Yes, tell us your preferred hotel, category, location or budget and we'll build around it, or recommend suitable options if you'd like our suggestions.

Q. Are there any hidden charges?

Ans: No. We share clear inclusions and exclusions before you confirm your booking, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

Q. How much time do I need to explore Kerala properly?

Ans: It depends on the destinations you want. A shorter trip can focus on Munnar and Alleppey; a longer holiday can add Thekkady, Kochi, Kovalam or Wayanad. We'll suggest a pace that fits your available days.

Q. How do I book my Kerala holiday?

Ans: Send us your travel dates, number of travellers, preferred destinations, hotel requirements and approximate budget. We'll prepare an itinerary and quotation, and you can confirm once you're happy with the plan.
