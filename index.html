FAQs about Kerala Tours

Q. When is the ideal time to visit Kerala?

Ans: Kerala is most pleasant from October to March. April and May turn warm, and the June–September monsoon brings heavy rain but also Kerala's most dramatic green landscapes.

Q. How long does a trip to Kerala usually take?

Ans: Most travellers spend 5–7 days covering a hill station, the backwaters and one coastal or heritage stop. Shorter 3–4 night trips work well if you focus on just one region.

Q. Can I customize my Kerala itinerary?

Ans: Yes, every package on this page is a starting point. We adjust destinations, pace, hotel category and activities to match your dates, budget and interests.

Q. Do you provide airport transfers?

Ans: All our packages include airport pickup and drop, along with private A/C transport between destinations for the length of your trip.

Q. Can you arrange honeymoon packages?

Ans: Yes, our honeymoon itineraries pair private houseboat stays with quieter hill and beach resorts, and we can arrange small romantic touches on request.

Q. Are hotels included in the packages?

Ans: Yes, accommodation is included at the category you choose, from comfortable 3-star stays to 4-star resorts and heritage properties.

Q. Can I book a private cab?

Ans: Yes, we arrange private A/C cabs with experienced local drivers for airport transfers, sightseeing and inter-city travel, either on their own or as part of a package.

Q. What local dishes should I try in Kerala?

Ans: Try Karimeen Pollichathu (pearl spot fish), appam with stew, puttu-kadala, and a traditional Sadya on a banana leaf. For dessert, don't miss Palada Payasam.

Q. Is it safe to travel alone in Kerala?

Ans: Kerala is generally considered safe for solo travellers during the day. As anywhere, it's best to avoid wandering alone late at night in unfamiliar areas.

Q. How do I get a customized quotation?

Ans: Share your travel dates, group size and interests through our enquiry form or WhatsApp, and we'll send a detailed itinerary and quote, usually within a day.
