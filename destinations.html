Destinations of Kerala

Six very different sides of the same state — pick one, or thread several together into a single route.

Cochin

1-2 days recommended

Kochi is famous for its 500-year colonial history, iconic Chinese fishing nets and Fort Kochi's art-filled lanes. Visitors love its laid-back harbour charm, fresh seafood and easy blend of Portuguese, Dutch and Indian heritage.

Plan a Trip Here

Munnar

2-3 days recommended

Munnar is Kerala's most famous hill station, known for its sprawling tea gardens, cool climate and the rare Nilgiri Tahr at Eravikulam National Park. Honeymooners and nature lovers flock here for the misty mountain views.

Plan a Trip Here

Thekkady

1-2 days recommended

Thekkady is home to Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, one of India's premier tiger and elephant reserves. Visitors come for boat safaris on Periyar Lake and guided walks through fragrant cardamom and pepper plantations.

Plan a Trip Here

Alleppey

1-2 days recommended

Known as the 'Venice of the East', Alleppey is Kerala's most iconic backwater destination. Its famous houseboat cruises through palm-fringed canals and the annual Nehru Trophy Boat Race draw travellers from across the world.

Plan a Trip Here

Kovalam

2 days recommended

Kovalam put Kerala on the international beach map in the 1970s with its three crescent-shaped beaches and historic lighthouse. It remains popular for Ayurvedic spas, seafood shacks and easy sunset walks.

Plan a Trip Here

Varkala

1-2 days recommended

Varkala is unique in Kerala for its dramatic cliffs rising straight from the Arabian Sea. Travellers love its cliffside cafes, the sacred Papanasam Beach believed to wash away sins, and a slower, laid-back pace.

Plan a Trip Here

Trivandrum

1 day recommended

As Kerala's capital, Thiruvananthapuram is famous for the historic Padmanabhaswamy Temple, one of the world's richest shrines, along with colonial-era museums and easy access to Kovalam's beaches.

Plan a Trip Here

Wayanad

2-3 days recommended

Wayanad draws visitors with its misty Western Ghats scenery, the ancient Edakkal Caves with prehistoric rock carvings, and sprawling coffee and spice plantations. It's a favourite for trekking and wildlife spotting.

Plan a Trip Here

Kozhikode

1 day recommended

Kozhikode marks the spot where Vasco da Gama first landed in India in 1498, opening the sea route from Europe. Today it's loved for its historic spice bazaars, famous halwa and relaxed beachfront promenade.

Plan a Trip Here

Kannur

1-2 days recommended

Kannur is the heartland of Theyyam, a centuries-old ritual dance-worship unique to North Kerala. Its quieter, less-crowded beaches and traditional handloom villages appeal to travellers seeking authentic culture.

Plan a Trip Here

Bekal

1 day recommended

Bekal is home to Kerala's largest fort, a 17th-century keyhole-shaped structure with sweeping views of the Arabian Sea. Its uncrowded beach and cinematic backdrop have made it a favourite filming location.

Plan a Trip Here

Kumarakom

1-2 days recommended

Kumarakom sits on Vembanad Lake, Kerala's largest lake, and is famous for its bird sanctuary drawing migratory species each winter. Its backwater resorts and houseboat cruises make it a favourite for a relaxed retreat.

Plan a Trip Here

Vagamon

1-2 days recommended

Vagamon is often called Kerala's 'mini Scotland' for its rolling green meadows and pine forests. It's popular with travellers looking for a quieter, less commercial hill-station experience, plus paragliding over the valleys.

Plan a Trip Here

Athirappilly

1 day recommended

Athirappilly is Kerala's largest waterfall, often called the 'Niagara of India', crashing through dense Sholayar forest. It's a favourite day trip and has featured in numerous Indian films.

Plan a Trip Here

Kollam

1 day recommended

Kollam sits on Ashtamudi Lake, the second-largest lake in Kerala and gateway to the backwaters. Known historically for its cashew and coir trade, it offers quieter houseboat cruises than the busier Alleppey routes.

Plan a Trip Here

Poovar

1 day recommended

Poovar is famous for the rare spot where a river, calm backwaters and the Arabian Sea all meet side by side. Its floating cottages and golden sandbar make it a quiet, romantic escape near the Tamil Nadu border.

Plan a Trip Here

Kasaragod

1 day recommended

Kasaragod, Kerala's northernmost district, is known for its unique multilingual culture and historic forts. Its uncrowded beaches and folk traditions like Theyyam offer a quieter, off-the-beaten-path Kerala.

Plan a Trip Here

Kottayam

1 day recommended

Kottayam is known as the 'land of letters, legends and latex' for its high literacy, ancient churches and vast rubber plantations. It's also a quiet gateway to the Vembanad backwaters and Kumarakom.

Plan a Trip Here

Idukki

1-2 days recommended

Idukki is home to one of Asia's highest arch dams, set among dramatic hills and dense forest. Its wildlife sanctuary, cardamom plantations and rugged terrain attract travellers looking for Kerala's wilder side.

Plan a Trip Here

Thrissur

1 day recommended

Thrissur is called Kerala's cultural capital, home to the spectacular Thrissur Pooram festival where decorated elephants parade through the streets. Its ancient Vadakkunnathan Temple is one of Kerala's oldest shrines.

Plan a Trip Here

Palakkad

1 day recommended

Palakkad is known as the 'rice bowl of Kerala', spread across a natural gap in the Western Ghats that brings cooler, drier weather. Its paddy fields, forts and agrarian landscape offer a different side of the state.

Plan a Trip Here

Kanyakumari

1 day recommended

Kanyakumari marks the southernmost tip of mainland India, where the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea and Indian Ocean meet. Travellers come for the famous sunrise-to-sunset views and the offshore Vivekananda Rock Memorial.

Plan a Trip Here

Rameshwaram

1-2 days recommended

Rameshwaram is one of Hinduism's four sacred Char Dham sites, famous for the Ramanathaswamy Temple with India's longest temple corridor. Its coastal roads and the Pamban Bridge draw pilgrims and travellers alike.

Plan a Trip Here
